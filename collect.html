📋 Official Collection Protocol (Read Before Collecting)

Goal: True phone distribution covering all real-world variations.

  • Lighting: 1/3 daylight, 1/3 LED/fluorescent room light, 1/3 warm/dim evening light.
  • Backgrounds: Wooden desk, bedsheet/fabric, held directly in fingers/hand.
  • Variations: Front side, back side, folded along middle, crumpled/worn, partial note cut off by frame edge.
  • Distance: Close-up (filling frame) vs far (note fills 40% of frame with surrounding table).
  • Rule: Keep Session A (Train) and Session B (Test) completely distinct. Never mix photos between the two.
